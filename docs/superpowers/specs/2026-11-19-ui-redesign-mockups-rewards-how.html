How scoring works

The score answers one question: does this person beat the market, or just pick favourites?

01Points per pick02Small samples03Points to score04New traders05What counts
1

Points per pick

When a trader posts a pick, we record the market's price at that moment. A $1 contract bought at that price pays $1 if the pick wins.

points = outcome − price

Someone who wins exactly as often as the odds say averages zero, however high their hit rate looks.

Favourite at 85¢$1 contract
paid 85¢15¢
WIN +15¢LOSE −85¢
Underdog at 35¢$1 contract
paid 35¢+65¢ if it wins
WIN +65¢LOSE −35¢
2

Shrinking small samples

A few lucky picks shouldn't top the board. We add 10 phantom picks worth zero to every record, so a short streak counts for little and a long track record counts for a lot.

3 lucky wins avg +50¢ → +11.5¢ after shrinking
40 picks, steady edge avg +15¢ → +12¢ after shrinking
WinLossPhantom pick (0 points)
3

From points to a score

The average edge is mapped onto 0 to 100. A score of 50 means no edge over the market. A sustained 15¢ edge per pick is about 88.

score = 50 + 50 · tanh(edge / 0.15)
4

New traders

Until a trader has 5 resolved picks, they show as NEW instead of a number. Too few picks would make any score noise.

NEW → 5 resolved picks → 47
5

What counts

Only picks we can price fairly at the moment they were posted.

✓Picks on markets we can price at the time of the post
✓The earliest post, if the same pick is posted twice
✕Voided markets are left out
▲ 16The 14-day arrow beside a score compares it with the score two weeks ago. It only shows for changes of 2 points or more.
